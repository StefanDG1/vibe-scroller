# VibeScroll

VibeScroll helps adults who own projects, including people who do not write code, turn useful things found while scrolling into knowledge they can retrieve, connect to their interests and apply to their projects. Users capture links, uploads or text, review evidence and main points, decide relevance, inspect a plan, separately approve bounded coding and follow a draft PR.
