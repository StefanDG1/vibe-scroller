# VibeScroll

VibeScroll helps adults who own projects, including people who do not write code, turn useful things found while scrolling into knowledge they can retrieve, connect to their interests and apply to their projects. Users capture links, uploads or text, review evidence and main points, decide relevance, inspect a plan, separately approve bounded coding and follow a draft PR. A useful library precedes project connection. Unavailable sources and no-fit decisions are valid results; publication, merge, deployment and benefit remain separate facts.

The owner selected VibeScroll as the product name and Scroll, an original soft illustrated paper creature, as the always-present guide in the normal shell and onboarding. The visual direction is Scroll's charcoal studio with green-gray surfaces, meaningful mint actions and warm amber artwork. Familiar navigation, readable evidence and clear next decisions retain the useful restraint of modern ChatGPT/Codex and ElevenLabs references without copying their brands or assets. The current theme is dark; a light theme is a separate optional decision. Motion can be turned off without hiding Scroll. Responsive core flows retain 44px controls, visible keyboard focus and reduced motion.
