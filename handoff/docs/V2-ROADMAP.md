# V2 business and marketing automation

Mode: explanation. V2 is proprietary, subscription-only, and excluded from V1 implementation.

## Product direction

V1 applies useful ideas to selected existing projects. V2 can also propose a new business, create its repository, configure customer-owned services, test it, deploy it, and support an authorized marketing workflow.

The underlying pain remains the same: a person can find and save useful ideas faster than they can apply them. "From vibe coder to vibe scroller" is a campaign concept. The product should sell specific work and informed experiments, not guaranteed wealth or passive income.

## Opportunity workflow

New saved content can trigger a low-cost screening pass. Cluster related insights and look for an existing project fit before proposing another business. Check whether the user already rejected or deferred the concept. Avoid sending a notification for every minor variation of the same idea.

A business proposal contains the source evidence, problem, target customer, competing approaches, product scope, technical feasibility, legal/platform constraints, acquisition hypothesis, cost range, pricing assumptions, and reasons not to build. Market claims need current research, not just the creator's confidence.

Send a safe notification with a link to review. The user can reject, edit, research further, or approve a bounded build. A source can lead to no action. The system does not create a new company or repo merely to make the dashboard look productive.

## Customer-owned accounts

Every generated business belongs to the customer. The customer owns its GitHub repository, hosting project, database, WorkOS environment, Stripe merchant account, email service, analytics, domain, and advertising accounts.

Exponential Education SRL bills only for VibeScroller. Its Stripe account must never receive the generated businesses' customer payments. Do not silently create a marketplace, merchant-of-record arrangement, or custody of customer business revenue.

The setup assistant can use official authorization flows and documented provider APIs. It can ask the user to create an account, complete verification, or authorize a project. A checklist distinguishes user-attested setup from a tested connection. Identity checks and KYC are completed by the actual owner.

A failed required connection keeps a generated app in setup or preview mode. It must not substitute VibeScroller's own payment identity, credentials, business address, or terms.

## Build and launch

After approval, create the repo from a maintained foundation, produce its product-specific specification, configure permitted accounts, implement tests, and deploy a preview. Run security, accessibility, billing, privacy, and domain checks before public launch.

A domain purchase, paid vendor subscription, or production activation requires explicit budget authority. A generated business cannot spend unlimited money because the platform subscription is active. Maintain separate budgets for development, hosting, email, content generation, and advertising.

The customer can operate through a cloud executor or their paired computer. The same permission, credential, isolation, revocation, and audit rules apply. Jobs pause when a required local device is offline.

## Marketing procedures

The owner's Mark Builds Brands source describes research, avatar development, offer definition, and necessary-beliefs work. Adapt that sequence to each SaaS rather than copying ecommerce claims unchanged. Keep the original source and owner-attested permission record. Do not claim endorsement or that every externally linked source was included.

The marketing system produces a research brief, audience evidence, offer, positioning, objections, truthful claims, landing-page copy, email drafts, SEO plan, content calendar, and experiment plan. Separate documented customer language from invented personas. Do not fabricate testimonials, reviews, case studies, or urgency.

Social publishing uses supported APIs and authorized accounts. Automated account creation is available only where a provider permits it. No CAPTCHA bypass, mass fake accounts, ban evasion, scraping of private audiences, or unsolicited spam.

Paid ads require a separately approved account, campaign, target audience, creatives, budget, billing route, and stop conditions. Track actual spend using provider records. A model estimate is not an enforceable ad-spend ceiling. Sensitive or regulated marketing requires an additional policy review.

## Automation modes

`Proposal mode` researches and drafts but waits for approval before creating external assets.

`Approved build mode` executes a specific approved business plan within its permitted accounts, budget, and launch scope.

`full retard mode` is the owner's exact requested internal label for the unattended saved-content-to-business path. Preserve the label in this roadmap. It means pre-authorized execution within a finite policy, not removal of safeguards. Public naming can be reviewed separately without changing this internal requirement.

The unattended mode must have explicit opt-in, eligible work categories, allowed providers, maximum concurrent businesses, per-business and account-wide spending caps, permitted publication actions, risk exclusions, daily summaries, and an emergency stop. Expensive, regulated, identity-verification, or unsupported actions still pause for the owner. It never invents consent or bypasses third-party controls.

## Rollout order

First add new-business proposals and repo generation. Then add verified customer-account provisioning and preview deployment. Add marketing drafts before automatic publication. Add paid advertising only after external spend enforcement and attribution are measured. Unattended mode is last.

Use demand and outcome evidence to decide when to proceed. A large number of generated repos without actual use is not a success metric. Track approved launches, active businesses, verified outcomes, costs, maintenance load, complaints, and shutdowns.

## Source and licence boundary

V1-authored MIT code can be reused under its licence. A proprietary V2 must preserve applicable notices and must not absorb a dependency whose licence is incompatible with the intended distribution. Convex's own backend licence is separate from the application's licence. Review all reused components before release.

The V1 repository should contain this roadmap and interface boundaries, not proprietary future implementation code or private third-party materials.
