# Staging Documentation: VibeScroller Reviewer Guide

This guide is for staging purposes only and has not been reviewed for production use.

## Introduction
The VibeScroller reviewer guide is designed to help reviewers understand the process of reviewing and troubleshooting VibeScroller issues.

## Inaccessible URLs
If a URL is inaccessible, it may be due to a number of reasons such as network issues or server errors. Reviewers should check the URL and ensure it is correct before attempting to access it.

## Permitted Upload/Transcript Recovery
Reviewers may need to recover uploaded transcripts or other files. This can be done by following the permitted upload and recovery procedures.

## Caption-Only Uncertainty
In some cases, reviewers may encounter caption-only uncertainty. This occurs when the transcript is not available, but the captions are. Reviewers should use their best judgment when reviewing caption-only content.

## Supplied Evidence Review
Reviewers should carefully review any supplied evidence, including transcripts, captions, and other supporting documents.

## Valid No-Fit Outcomes
A valid no-fit outcome occurs when the reviewer determines that the content does not fit the required criteria. Reviewers should document their reasoning and provide feedback to the submitter.

## Correction Before Rematching
If a correction is needed, reviewers should correct the issue before rematching the content.

## Exact Bounded Plan Approval
All plans must be approved before coding. Reviewers should ensure that the plan is exact and bounded before approving it.

## Upload, Transcript, Caption, No-Fit, and Approval
The following terms are important to understand when reviewing VibeScroller content:
* Upload: The process of submitting content for review.
* Transcript: A written record of the content.
* Caption: A text description of the content.
* No-Fit: A determination that the content does not fit the required criteria.
* Approval: The process of approving a plan or content for use.

By following this guide, reviewers can ensure that they are properly reviewing and troubleshooting VibeScroller issues.
