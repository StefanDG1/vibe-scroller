# Landing-page content

The site introduces a free MIT-licensed SaaS template, explains accounts/organizations/billing, presents five design directions, describes the setup, and answers practical questions. Its primary CTA opens the public GitHub repository. Documentation describes actual commands and required provider accounts.

Routes are `/`, `/designs`, `/designs/[recipe]`, `/docs`, and `/privacy`. The root uses the selected Cobalt design. Alternative landing pages are marked as design options and excluded from indexing. The starter is excluded from indexing and has its own product identity.

The site does not collect leads, send messages, charge customers, or embed analytics. Its application preview is labeled illustrative. Future paid services are described as future work, without pricing or availability claims. Final visual refinement remains an owner decision.

Before promoting a release, ensure every advertised feature matches `docs/status.md`. Verify repository links, canonical URLs, metadata, responsive layouts, focus states, and form behavior. A public business site must not suggest that demo screenshots are real customer data.
