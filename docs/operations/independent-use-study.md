# Observe independent use and repeat value

Mode: how-to. Preparation for P05/P06/P08/P09/P11; no participant or payment outcome is supplied by this protocol.

Keep identities, consent, source references and observations in ignored private study records. Use one opaque participant ID across usability and the rolling cohort; never duplicate someone who continues. Record enrolment, audience/path, own-account confirmation, OS/device, version, analytics consent, voluntary-feedback consent and withdrawal. Recruitment/contact/publication needs its specific owner authorization; this task sends none.

## Observe the first-use tasks

Hosted participants start with their own account and one permitted source. Ask them to import, inspect evidence and retrieve the insight again without mandatory GitHub. Observe optional selected-project review and a plan or correct no-fit decision separately. Record prompts/help exactly; assisted completion is not independent success.

Exercise empty/loading/error/unavailable states, exhausted allowance, disconnected provider and offline paired laptop. Preserve input and read authoritative state before retrying an uncertain write. Record keyboard, visible focus, reduced motion, touch targets and long text at 320 through desktop widths. Native browser emulation and physical Android are separate fields.

Five fresh hosted participants supply P06's proposed small-pilot denominator; at least four complete import/retrieval unaided after fixes. A separate project-review observation checks understanding. Two clean-checkout personal testers supply P05, including one retained-data upgrade. Record active time, approval waits, billed resources and help separately. Staff/owner browser regression does not count as a fresh participant.

## Continue only within capacity

P02 forecasts precede admission. Start with three rolling users; expand only after P06/P07 and capacity acceptance. No ceiling increase is assumed. Preserve failed retrieval, duplicate, unreviewed, opt-out, decline and dropout outcomes. Keep end-to-end retrieval denominators distinct from attempted-analysis completion.

Record activation timestamps separately: library activation requires opening a completed analysis; project activation requires a relevance decision plus saved plan, manual handoff or documented no action. A return later in the same week is not later-week repeat value. Each four-week window starts at that participant's actual activation. A positive judgment requires their explicit report, not a merge or lack of complaints.

Weekly records include versions, interventions, correction burden, unsupported sources, costs/support effort and voluntary return/value statements. Compare matched manual tasks where consent permits; difficulty, assistance and funding/model differences remain visible. Include every enrollee and disclose observation windows. Five of the first ten activated users reporting later-week value is a decision aid, not population market-fit evidence.

P09 observes genuine independent voluntary buyers, reasons to buy/decline and at least one applicable renewal opportunity. Annual prepayment is not renewal. Founder/friend-support purchases and sandbox transactions do not prove demand. Reconcile entitlements, grants, fees, refunds and accounting without manufacturing charges. Prices/allowances remain unchanged.
