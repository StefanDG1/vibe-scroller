Mode: how-to. P02 retains the EUR 10 inference pilot and all existing service/compute ceilings. Reservations for unknown usage remain reserved.

1. Export authoritative job/attempt receipts and reservations read-only into ignored `private/`. Include failed attempts, retries, teardown, matching, storage and egress, not only successful analysis.
2. Obtain provider invoices or authoritative period-complete billing exports for the dedicated VibeScroller projects. Record billing period, currency, gross use, promotional/included credits and cash charges separately. Do not accept a shared-account zero as per-job cost.
3. Join exported lines to workload IDs. Allocate shared expenses explicitly; unjoinable charges stay unattributed. Keep original evidence reference and allocation basis privately. A missing/delayed provider period stays pending.
4. Prepare the strict `costInput` in `packages/evaluation/cost-reconciliation.ts`, then run `node scripts/reconcile-costs.ts private/path/cost-input.json outputs/new-cost-report.json`. The report excludes workload/line IDs. Duplicate identities, foreign mappings, wrong periods, inconsistent cash/gross/credit totals and unsafe integer totals are invalid. Zero cash with included credits retains gross economic use.
5. Reconcile currency totals through the documented dated FX policy; do not sum currencies. The initial helper keeps currencies separate and does not calculate operating margin. Fees, Billing/Managed Payments on the actual basis, Radar, refunds and FX come from authoritative records plus [the existing financial model](../../finance/assumptions.json). Run `python finance/test_model.py` and `python finance/calculate.py` for model checks, not invoice proof.
6. Forecast the proposed cohort workload against inference, service and sandbox headroom after spent and all reservations. Include failure/retry bursts and storage/egress limits. A passing forecast does not authorize a purchase, cap change or recruitment. No admission is ready while required invoices/capacity remain missing or unattributed.

Exit 1 is invalid input/output. Exit 2 is pending reconciliation/capacity; exit 0 means supplied records meet the mechanical conditions, subject to operator verification of evidence and allocation. The owner-defined 60-percent operating margin excludes salary, marketing, development and company tax. It is not all-in profit and is not proven by quotes or conservative inference estimates.
