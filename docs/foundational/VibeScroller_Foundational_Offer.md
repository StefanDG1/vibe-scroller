# VibeScroller foundational offer

VibeScroller's proposed offer is a mobile browser library that helps a developer review saved ideas against selected projects, prepare an editable plan, and authorize a bounded draft-PR workflow. Lead with project relevance and evidence. A video summary or coding agent alone is already available elsewhere.

This completes the original Mark Builds Brands Offer Brief Template after the [research](VibeScroller_Foundational_Research.md) and [avatar](VibeScroller_Foundational_Avatar.md). Product specifications define the intended package; they do not certify that every integration is enabled. The [implementation status](../implementation-status.md) controls claims about tested behavior. Latest owner funding steering and known gates are recorded in the [ledger](README.md).

## Potential product name ideas

## Level of consciousness

High awareness of the immediate workflow is the useful hypothesis. The person can identify a saved source and an active project. Awareness of a dedicated source-to-project product may be low. This is a qualitative positioning judgment, not an observed segmentation result.

Avoid explaining ordinary developers' lives back to them. Show a concrete source, what it recommends, what the repository already does, and what decision follows.

## Level of awareness

The primary candidate is problem-aware or solution-aware. They know about bookmarks, video summaries, notebooks, or coding agents. They need a reason to add VibeScroller to that stack. Product-aware and most-aware customers cannot be assumed before the product has customer evidence.

Library-only prospects may seek retrieval and summaries. They should see that useful entry point without being forced to connect a repository. The eventual pricing case for them needs separate validation because close alternatives already sell video search.

## Stage of sophistication

The surrounding market is crowded with bookmark tools, source-grounded assistants, and agents that plan and open PRs. A numbered sophistication stage is not measured. Treat the audience as familiar with AI claims and skeptical of automatic productivity promises.

Show continuity of evidence and decisions. Do not claim the world's first video-to-code system, the only agent with approval, or exclusive repository-aware planning. The current GitHub cloud-agent documentation already includes research, planning, iteration, and PR creation. [GitHub documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/agents/cloud-agent/about-cloud-agent)

## Big idea

"A saved idea becomes useful when you can decide what it means for the project you are building."

This is the proposed argument. A clip can be interesting and still irrelevant, already implemented, or risky. VibeScroller aims to help make that distinction, preserve the source, and prepare a next step the user can inspect. Whether the service does this better than a manual stack remains a pilot question.

## Metaphor

Use a learning journal that follows an idea into a project. The journal records the source, the decision, the plan, and the observed outcome. Explain the actual workflow immediately after the metaphor. Do not suggest the software learns or makes business judgments autonomously because the metaphor sounds appealing.

## Potential unique mechanism of the problem

The proposed mechanism is a missing project-review step. A saved link remembers the source. It does not by itself evaluate the technique against the current repository, priorities, and constraints. A generic summary can preserve useful information while leaving that work with the user.

This is a hypothesis about the proposed segment. It is not proof that bookmarks fail, a universal cause of inactivity, or a psychological diagnosis. Public dissent shows some people do not want every save turned into work.

## Potential unique mechanism of the solution

The proposed mechanism is an evidence-linked review against a selected repository snapshot. The service keeps the source and bounded transcript or frame evidence, compares the idea with recorded project context, and explains a possible change or a reason to decline. It then creates a plan the user can edit. Separate approval authorizes coding with a specified executor and funding ceiling. A draft PR preserves the implementation for review.

The distinction is the complete source-to-outcome record. Its uniqueness is unverified and its superiority is unmeasured. V1's MIT intent also makes "secret proprietary method" a poor description. Market the inspectable workflow, not an invented technical monopoly.

## Guru

No guru is required. The source author may be credible, mistaken, or selling something. VibeScroller must retain the distinction between a creator's claim and the system's interpretation. Mark Builds Brands provides the supplied research process, with owner-attested permission; that does not make the author a VibeScroller adviser, partner, endorser, or source of software performance evidence.

## Discovery story

The documented origin is the owner's interest in applying useful saved content to projects and keeping a learning record. The starting workload is one to five daily English videos. The owner wanted mobile access, selected-repository context, an editable plan, explicit coding permission, and draft PR status.

Do not expand that into an invented story about hundreds of customers, wasted hours, income, a life-changing launch, or a successful transformation. A founder story can describe why the workflow was specified. It cannot replace customer proof.

## Product

### Intended core package

The library accepts permitted video URLs, uploads, and text. The user sees processing status, evidence-supported summaries and main points, and explicit access failures. They can search, tag, edit, export, and delete records. They may use the library before connecting GitHub. Same-workspace deduplication avoids repeating a completed analysis charge for an identical source under the defined policy.

For selected projects, repository context and a project profile help evaluate plausible matches. The review explains current behavior, evidence, proposed change, risks, effort, and alternatives. No fit, already implemented, unsupported, and deferred are valid results. Accepting a proposal produces a versioned editable plan. Execution approval is separate and includes repository, base commit, plan version, executor, funding route, and maximum spend.

Authorized coding runs in a verified isolated environment. The publisher creates a draft PR; GitHub events update its state. The user retains responsibility for review and merge. A merged PR and a confirmed benefit are recorded separately. The product does not merge, deploy, create businesses, or run advertising in V1.

For a paid remotely hosted product, current official commercial access requires the interest or waitlist process. Access is not granted here. Do not offer an enabled SIWC button or imply that connecting identity unlocks all inference.

An independently authored adapter to the documented protocol is implementation work; it does not remove commercial service-access gates. This licence and the service access terms are distinct requirements.

### Risk reversal and proof

The proposed preview lets users inspect a real output before subscribing. Plans can be reviewed and exported before execution. Bounded quotes, explicit approval, selected repository access, and honest failures reduce uncertainty. They do not guarantee correctness, business results, or an accepted PR.

Publish cancellation, refund, retention, and consumer-rights terms only after operator and legal evidence is confirmed. Do not invent a money-back guarantee or use the preview to waive statutory rights. The intended Exponential Education operator and tax position still need official records. Physical Android verification is deferred to V1.1 by the user and must not be marked passed.

## Potential headline and subheadline ideas

The existing lead is "Turn saved videos into changes worth building." Support it with "Keep the source, check the fit to your project, and review a plan before approving code." This is a copy proposal subject to enabled-capability checks.

Alternative concepts are "Review the ideas you saved against the project you are building" and "From saved source to a plan you can inspect." A library-first line is "Keep useful sources in one place and decide what to do next." None is a validated winning headline.

Keep "Start your library" as the primary initial action. Use "See the workflow" for a rights-cleared demonstration. A coding action should be specific to the selected plan and maximum budget, rather than a vague "make it happen" button.

## Belief chains

The argument starts with a personally relevant problem, then shows why project context matters, why evidence helps judge a recommendation, why the proposed workflow is worth trying, why permission and cost remain bounded, and why the price could be worthwhile after inspecting an output. The exact six first-person statements appear in the [belief document](VibeScroller_Foundational_Beliefs.md).

Each step needs proof proportionate to the claim. A product diagram can explain sequence. A rights-cleared demonstration can show a specific result. A pilot can measure correction burden and usefulness. None replaces the next level of evidence.

## Funnel architecture

1. Show the specific source-to-project problem and a labeled demonstration.
2. Explain capture, evidence, fit, plan review, and separate execution approval. Include a no-fit example.
3. Let the user start a library before GitHub or AI-provider setup.
4. Offer the capped preview with clear supported inputs and availability.
5. Invite selected-repository context when the person wants project relevance.
6. Show the reviewable output, the actual pricing and limits, and the applicable terms.
7. Quote each funded action and obtain coding approval separately.
8. Track the draft PR and later feedback without treating merge as benefit.

This is a proposed product funnel. No ad campaign, account creation, paid media, live checkout activation, or external publication is authorized by this document. The supplied guide's Shopify theme, ad-budget rules, targeting countries, and ecommerce margins do not transfer to this EU-first software offer.
