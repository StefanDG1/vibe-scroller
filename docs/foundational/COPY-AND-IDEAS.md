# Review copy and evolve the foundational documents

Mode: how-to. These documents are a working copy brief, not immutable research or proof of demand.

## Use the brief for every copy task

Read [Research](VibeScroller_Foundational_Research.md), [Avatar](VibeScroller_Foundational_Avatar.md), [Offer](VibeScroller_Foundational_Offer.md) and [Beliefs](VibeScroller_Foundational_Beliefs.md). Identify the reader, task, objection and evidence behind the proposed claim. Compare feature, funding, price and permission claims with [implementation status](../implementation-status.md), contracts and owner decisions.

Marketing can explain a supported benefit; it cannot convert a hypothesis into a measured result. Error messages, approval text and billing disclosures must remain precise even when stronger language might sell better.

## Add and review an idea

Add an entry to [the idea backlog](idea-backlog.md), or tell Codex: "Review this marketing idea against all four foundational documents, record conflicts and suggest changes before applying it: …" Both routes use the same review.

Record the source/date, intended audience, proposed message or feature, evidence, affected sections and requested decision. Keep private notes, account information and proprietary source material in ignored storage. Summarize only permitted evidence here.

| Check    | What to record                                                                                          |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Research | Supporting sources, contrary evidence, missing evidence and fact versus hypothesis                      |
| Avatar   | Existing behavior or objection addressed; a new segment needs validation                                |
| Offer    | What the reader receives now, cost implication and unavailable features                                 |
| Beliefs  | Which of the six beliefs it supports or contradicts                                                     |
| Product  | Actual enabled routes, permissions, tests and provider/legal limits                                     |
| Decision | Compatible, conflicts, needs evidence, accepted for a test, adopted, deferred or rejected, with reasons |

Compatibility is not market validation. Record a concrete test with success/failure criteria where evidence is missing, and preserve a counterargument. Changes to scope, price, permission, funding, guarantees or audience need an owner decision before customer-facing copy uses them. Ordinary edits within the agreed brief can proceed without another approval.

## Update the documents together

When the decision and evidence support an update, change affected sections and cite the evidence/date. Distinguish observations from interpretations. Check the other three documents for contradictions, update dependent claims, and append [the change log](change-log.md) with the idea ID, sections, evidence and remaining uncertainty. Git history preserves earlier versions. Do not rewrite old observations as though a newer feature existed at the time.

After a meaningful product, price or provider change, review affected website, app, onboarding, email and SEO copy. After a research test, record positive and negative results. This process runs when an idea or evidence arrives; no automatic campaign or background monitoring is implied.

## Reusable review prompt

> Read AGENTS.md, all four foundational documents, their change log, the idea backlog and current implementation status. Review the following owner idea as a proposal, not permission to make unsupported claims. Identify supporting and contrary evidence, conflicts with each document, product/provider/legal constraints, affected copy, and a concrete validation test where necessary. Record an idea ID and recommendation. Apply routine compatible edits; seek an owner decision for changes to scope, price, permission, funding, guarantees or audience. Update affected documents and their change log together only when justified. Preserve uncertainty and dissent. Idea: [paste the idea here].
