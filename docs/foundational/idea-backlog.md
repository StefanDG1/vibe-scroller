## F001: User-selected ChatGPT plan funding

Source: owner's September 30 request. Audience: the current developer/founder segment with an eligible plan. Proposed message: "Use your connected ChatGPT plan for eligible tasks when available. Review its limits and choose the funding route before work starts."

| Check    | Review                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Research | Official protocol supports sharing; an owner-authorized local text response completed. No measured customer saving, conversion or retention evidence        |
| Avatar   | Addresses cost/control objections for people with an eligible plan; do not assume all prospects have one                                                    |
| Offer    | Local text utility works. Browser-connected dispatch and paid hosted access are unavailable. Transcription, storage and cloud execution have separate costs |
| Beliefs  | Supports choice/control and value. Unlimited or costless use would contradict limits                                                                        |
| Product  | Preference is separate from OAuth consent and approved job funding. No founder subscription pooling, silent fallback or unisolated coding                   |
| Decision | Adopt the conditional direction. Defer hosted activation and savings claims until eligibility and cost evidence exist                                       |

Validation: test permitted workloads, revocation and exhausted allowance, then measure route costs under the permitted commercial contract. Success requires completed requests with correct attribution and no alternate charge; unavailable scope, incomplete output or unwanted fallback fails. Shared plan limits can make the route unavailable. Do not promise a discount that billing does not implement.
