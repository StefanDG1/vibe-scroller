# Separate Stripe account and prepare Managed Payments

Status: integration preparation; provider activation and live tests pending. Date: 2026-10-02.

The owner authorized a dedicated VibeScroller Stripe account under the existing login and Exponential Education SRL legal entity. The new Romanian account is separate from the education platform's account. Existing education products, keys, webhooks and subscriptions are not migrated or modified. Stripe's legal-entity reuse flow was selected. Account creation and access to its live dashboard are observed; these do not establish charge/payout readiness or Managed Payments approval.

The owner subsequently selected Stripe Managed Payments if eligible and accepted its additional 3.5% transaction fee. This becomes the preferred launch route. It does not authorize optional paid Radar tiers, climate contributions, a real-money test or unrelated purchases. Romania and automated SaaS are supported by the current official documentation, but the account and product still require provider eligibility and terms acceptance.

The provisioning command requires an expected account ID and matching key/mode before creating anything; live mode additionally requires an explicit --live flag. It reads the credential's own account using accounts.retrieve(null). Version 1.1.0 creates separate Starter, Pro and processing-credit products, six recurring EUR prices and two one-time prices. Existing lookups are validated before mutation. Repeated runs reuse the same objects; conflicting lookups fail. Version 1.0.0 products remain untouched. Tax-inclusive amounts preserve the approved catalogue. The initial product classification is SaaS personal use (txcd_10103000), reflecting the initial consumer offer; this is neither a declaration of the seller's VAT registration nor provider eligibility evidence.

Checkout explicitly chooses direct or managed processing. Managed Payments omits automatic_tax, adaptive_pricing and customer_update, which Stripe controls. It does not fall back to direct charging on refusal. Live Managed Payments additionally requires STRIPE_MANAGED_PAYMENTS_VERIFIED=true and the existing live-checkout release switch. The configured country allowlist still applies before session creation. Direct processing retains its official tax-evidence gate and explicitly opts out of dashboard Managed Payments defaults. The collected Stripe billing location and customer type still need production acceptance checks; a frontend country selection alone is not proof of either.

Managed Payments customer invoices and transaction support come through Link in covered jurisdictions. Do not issue a second Exponential consumer tax invoice by assuming it remains the transaction's merchant of record. The accountant still receives company accounting records, provider fee documents, withheld-tax breakdowns and payout reconciliation. A separate account does not create a separate company or reset company-wide tax obligations. The former below-threshold direct-sale strategy remains an alternative, not an automatically enabled fallback.

Reconciliation checks the credential's own account and customer existence. Confirmed provider deletion ends subscription allowance pools and leaves purchased credits, historical ledgers, workspace membership and the library intact. A tombstone blocks stale subscription observations; paid-period grants bind the applied billing revision. A later checkout can create a new Stripe customer. Missing-resource confirmation is distinct from permission errors or outages. The hourly reconciliation also covers deletion without a webhook. These are tested application behaviors, not evidence that Stripe's full Link deletion journey has been exercised.

Managed Payments subscriptions do not enter the direct seller's Romanian invoice-submission queue. Provider accounting exports, fee and withheld-tax reporting still need a separate reviewed procedure. The code preparation does not close actual provider checkout/renewal/refund tests, supported plan-change validation, the end-to-end Link deletion journey or final legal publication. Existing subscriptions cannot be converted into Managed Payments subscriptions. No paid V1 completion or legal-review claim follows from this ADR.
