# ADR 098: Insight presentation and remembered Library position

Status: accepted owner direction, October 9, 2026. Release evidence is separate.

## Decision

Reuse the insight title already authored by analysis. Ask future analyses for a short descriptive title and a semantic icon from a fixed contract whitelist. Accept historical clients without that optional field and persist a deterministic fallback at new completion. Display legacy records with a stable fallback without rewriting evidence, claims, processing generations or historical records. Icons express a subject, never verified confidence.

Category names stay on one line with ellipsis and their complete accessible name. Closed branches lose their active treatment. The current filing root opens by default. Resume the authorized last topic, representation, filing, closed branch, expanded insight and bounded scroll position. Store identifiers and geometry only under workspace/scope browser preferences, validate them against the newly authorized page and clear them on access loss. Missing or revoked records fall back to the available overview.

A selected topic may read one bounded current journey page, shared by its insight cards, to display proposal badges. This supersedes ADR097's click-only journey read. No per-card query, whole-table scan, automatic pagination, inference or new polling is introduced. Only drafts citing the exact source/insight/generation/revision may appear. Exclude rejected, deleted and observed completed proposals; label counts as proposals on the loaded page and use a plus when additional evaluations exist. Unknown external issue closure is not asserted. Opening an insight reveals proposal title, project and a bounded description; the existing authorized exact-draft route opens its full review. No connection is coding, publication or spending approval.

Keep the five bottom actions and map nested pages to their related destination. Center the Save control. Small standalone explanations use an accessible circle-help disclosure; essential warnings and task controls remain visible.

## Verification

Require authorization/version filtering tests, legacy and invalid-icon contract tests, bounded preference restoration, viewport checks, native collapse/highlight and remembered-place recovery, exact CI and separate canonical/provider evidence. Physical-device smoothness and independent comprehension require actual evidence. No model/API-token reanalysis or financial setting change is authorized by this refinement.
