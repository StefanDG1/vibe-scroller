# Review of the plan after V1

Mode: reference. Reviewed October 3, 2026. Reviewer: the implementing assistant. This is a structured self-review, not an independent audit or external product validation.

## Review result

The [plan](POST-V1-PLAN.md) has a workable sequence after the corrections below. Existing V1 acceptance and documentation precede broad distribution. Independent public-source setup and hosted onboarding have different tests. The next feature follows observed friction. V2 requires a separate decision.

Planning is complete, but the plan's implementation has not started. No application, policy, provider configuration or live checkout setting changes form part of this task. Timing and cohort targets are proposed decision aids; they are not forecasts or measured product-market fit.

## Unresolved decisions and dependencies

Two further source checks changed the draft. The actual evaluator can return exit 0 while settled costs remain null. P01 now states that quality closure cannot pass P02 or AC53 cost calibration. An exit code cannot turn unknown costs into settled evidence.

A strict interpretation of marketing dependencies could make recruitment wait for the cohort's own completed findings. P11 now permits an initial bounded, authorized demonstration and recruitment post after their capability, privacy and capacity checks. Broader distribution still waits for recurring-value evidence.

The synthetic collection also includes related scenario variants. P01 now groups those variants in the same development or held-out split and reports independent scenario counts. The rubric distinguishes faithful source reporting from the truth of the creator's claim.

Separate usability and retention exercises could unnecessarily recruit and bill two small groups. P08 now allows consenting usability participants to continue in one rolling cohort. Participants are counted once, assistance remains visible and each observation window starts at recorded activation. Expansion still waits for usability and measurement acceptance.

The initial technical audience remains a working recommendation. A different primary audience changes the cohort, onboarding tasks, paid offer and marketing assets together. Broad Food/Fashion library categories alone do not establish a new paying segment.

Supported personal deployment needs a follow-on scope decision. The first implementation estimate follows the provider/setup audit, rather than assuming the existing exported starter already supplies a complete distribution. No new framework is required by the plan.

Numerical annotation needs actual human scores. The existing approvals remain accepted and are not requested again. A human reviewer can score retained cases with the prepared rubric; the founder need not supply more archives or transcripts merely to start this work.

Provider cost verification depends on billing records and their reporting period. If invoices have not arrived, documentation, reviewer preparation, setup research and bounded usability work can continue. Missing cost data cannot justify unlimited onboarding or a guaranteed-margin claim.

Physical Android participation is needed for hardware acceptance when P10 capture work is selected. The laptop-assisted personal route remains distinct from unsupported hosted commercial ChatGPT use. Provider eligibility is rechecked before advertising a new route.

## Review disposition

No circular dependency requires new feature development before the cohort can begin. Documentation and acceptance preparation can overlap, while broad distribution waits for the relevant evidence and capacity. The plan leaves refusal, narrower scope and stopping as valid outcomes.

The remaining uncertainties concern reviewer availability, independently measured setup effort, delayed billing records, customer demand and explicit follow-on decisions. They are visible planning dependencies. They are not hidden promises, completed work or reasons to replace the selected stack.

Documentation checks and publishing outcomes for this planning task are recorded in [the implementation status](implementation-status.md). The review does not certify legal compliance, provider rights or future implementation quality.
